# Pharma Sales Analysis Dashboard

An interactive Power BI dashboard for exploring pharmaceutical sales data and identifying business insights.

## Dashboard Pages

- **Sales Overview:** Sales trends, yearly performance, sales channels, and sub-channels
- **Product Performance:** Top products, product classes, quantity, and average price
- **Geography & Team:** City-level sales, manager performance, and sales team quantities
- **Insights & Recommendations:** Key findings and suggested business actions

## Key Insights

- Total sales: **12.09bn**
- Total quantity: **29.39M**
- Average price: **412.21**
- Pharmacy contributes **52.7%** of sales; Hospital contributes **47.3%**
- Sales peaked in 2018 and declined in 2019 and 2020
- Ionclotide is the top product shown by sales

## Tools Used

- Microsoft Power BI
- Data source:- kaggle

## How to View

1. Download `Pharma_Project.pbix` from this repository.
2. Open it with Microsoft Power BI Desktop.
3. Use the Year and Country filters to explore the report.
